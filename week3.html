Week 3 Recap

The season is starting to get fucking real.

Three weeks in. The pretenders are starting to reveal themselves.

Matt and Evan are both 3-0.

Adam and Kenny are both 0-3.

And somewhere in between, seven managers are sitting at 2-1, 1-2, or generally wondering what the fuck is happening.

Week 3 gave us a 154.24-point monster performance, a 49.50-point ass beating, two teams getting their first wins, and one manager finally escaping the fucking basement.
